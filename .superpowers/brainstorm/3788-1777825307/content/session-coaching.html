Live Ride Intelligence: Session Timing

How should ARI "coach" the rider as they approach their session limit?

A

Fixed Threshold Alerts

Traditional alerts at milestones (e.g., 10m, 5m, 1m). Simple and predictable.

B

Spatial-Aware Coaching

The "nudge" depends on location. If you have 5m left but are 10m from the nearest hub, ARI starts alerting now.

C

Auto-Pivot to Drop-off

As the limit nears, the map UI pivots to "Return Mode", highlighting the most efficient hubs that fit your time window.
