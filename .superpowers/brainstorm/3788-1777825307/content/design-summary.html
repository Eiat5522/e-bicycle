Design Summary: ARI for Standard Bicycles

Core Strategy

The "Adaptive Ride Intelligence" (ARI) pivots from battery management to Session & Logistics Optimization. The goal is to ensure riders return bikes to physical hubs within their allotted time window to avoid penalties.
