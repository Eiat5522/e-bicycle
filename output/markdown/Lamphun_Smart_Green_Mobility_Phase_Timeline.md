## Slide 1 - Cover

### Lamphun Smart Green Mobility

**Phase 1 and Phase 2 Delivery Timeline**

Phase 1 focuses on public launch readiness by 30 September 2026. Phase 2 adds customer, tourism, connected-fleet, dashboard, API, and optimization enhancements through March 2027.

**Operating principle:** Operation First + App Ready + No Single Point of Failure.

## Slide 2 - Two-phase delivery at a glance

### Phase 1 - Must-have launch MVP

**Timeline:** 29 June-30 September 2026

Deliver the operational platform required to run 100 e-bikes with staff workflows, payment references, maintenance controls, reports, carbon estimates, and tested backup workflows.

### Phase 2 - Enhancements + scale

**Timeline:** 1 October 2026-31 March 2027

Use live operating data to improve customer experience, integrate connected fleet data, expand tourism programs, build deeper dashboards, and introduce optimization support.

**Gate between phases:** Phase 2 should start only after rental, return, payment, maintenance, battery, incident, report, and fallback workflows are stable in live use.

## Slide 3 - Phase 1 delivery timeline

Must-have features to launch real operations by 30 September 2026.

- **Weeks 1-2 - Discovery + model lock:** Confirm station workflows, user roles, data model, report definitions, fallback forms, launch KPIs, and acceptance criteria.
- **Weeks 3-5 - Core platform build:** Build customer, fleet, rental, payment reference, staff permissions, and database foundations.
- **Weeks 6-7 - Operations modules:** Build maintenance, battery, incident, spare parts, QR labels, evidence capture, and fallback workflow links.
- **Weeks 8-9 - Dashboard + reports:** Build daily/monthly reports, dashboard MVP, carbon estimate, revenue reconciliation, exports, and audit views.
- **Weeks 10-11 - Pilot + readiness:** Run station pilot, train administrators and technicians, test outage scenarios, and reconcile fallback records.
- **Weeks 12-13 - Launch hardening:** Fix pilot defects, complete security and data checks, finalize SOPs, freeze launch scope, and prepare go-live support.

## Slide 4 - Phase 1 feature breakdown

These workstreams define the MVP scope. The common acceptance test is operational traceability: every rental, payment, asset, incident, maintenance, and fallback action must be reviewable.

- **Rental operations:** Customer, fleet, QR asset control, check-out/check-in, battery state, photos, damage reports.
- **Financial traceability:** Thai QR / PromptPay, card/cash references, receipts, status verification.
- **Asset reliability:** Battery ID, charging-room record, PM schedule, work orders, repair history, spare parts usage.
- **Governance:** Staff permissions, incident records, exports, audit trail, daily and monthly reports.
- **Carbon reporting:** Distance accumulation, conservative carbon estimate, daily/monthly/yearly report exports.
- **Continuity workflow:** Google Forms, Sheets, Drive evidence, and manual QR payment log for outage scenarios.

## Slide 5 - Phase 2 delivery timeline

Enhancements follow live evidence, API readiness, and staff capacity.

- **Oct-Nov 2026 - Customer app enhancements:** Self-service booking, route map, notifications, rental reminders, feedback, and UX improvements.
- **Nov-Dec 2026 - Tourism promotion:** Green Passport, digital stamps, hotel voucher flow, partner routes, QR check-in, and route completion badges.
- **Dec-Jan 2027 - Connected fleet:** GPS, trip history, smart lock or IoT, geofence alerts, and battery monitoring where vendor APIs are ready.
- **Jan-Feb 2027 - Reliability analytics:** Maintenance alerts, repeat failure reports, technician performance, battery health, and rotation optimization.
- **Feb-Mar 2027 - Dashboards + APIs:** Executive, operations, fleet, financial, tourism, carbon, smart-city dashboards, and partner / IoT / payment APIs.
- **Mar 2027 onward - Optimization layer:** Demand forecast, fleet rebalancing recommendation, predictive maintenance, dynamic staffing, and tourism recommendation.

## Slide 6 - Phase 2 enhancement tracks

- **Tourism layer:** Green Passport, Green Stay, partner routes, QR check-ins, vouchers, and route badges.
- **Connected fleet:** GPS, smart lock, IoT, geofence, vehicle status, and battery monitoring integrations.
- **Dashboard suite:** Executive, operations, fleet, maintenance, financial, tourism, carbon, and mobility dashboards.
- **Optimization:** Demand forecast, rebalancing, predictive maintenance, battery rotation, and dynamic staffing.

**Critical control:** Do not add GPS, IoT, AI, carbon claims, or public dashboards until the Phase 1 dataset is complete enough to support reliable decisions.

## Slide 7 - Decision gates and launch readiness

1. **Launch MVP:** All 100 e-bikes and batteries registered; staff can run rental, return, payment reference, maintenance, battery, incident, and reports.
2. **Continuity proven:** Backup Google Forms, Sheets, Drive evidence, and manual QR payment record are tested through an outage simulation.
3. **Phase 2 start:** Data completeness, workflow stability, staff readiness, and vendor API access are confirmed.
4. **Scale + optimize:** Dashboards are used in real decisions; carbon claims are evidence-based; connected fleet data improves operations.

The timeline is intentionally phased: stabilize operations first, then add richer customer, tourism, connected-fleet, and analytics layers.
